About Swarm Field Theory

Swarm Field Theory is an exploratory theoretical framework investigating whether familiar physical behaviour can emerge from a simpler underlying system of interacting elements.

Rather than beginning with the particles and fields of established physics as fundamental objects, the work asks whether persistence, propagation, geometry, matter-like structures, and gravitational behaviour might arise from the dynamics of a more elementary substrate.

The purpose of this site is to provide a permanent home for papers, calculations, revisions, and supporting material developed as part of that investigation.
