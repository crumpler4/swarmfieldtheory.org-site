What this page describes This page describes the geometric language used in Swarm Field Theory. The language is intentionally physical because it helps make the mathematics easier to see: surface tension represents stored field capacity, membrane intersections represent propagation channels, curvature represents strain, and helical closure represents quantized cyclic behaviour. Zero Nodes and Coherence Membranes In the geometric model, a Zero Node is an irreducible null boundary. It is not a particle sitting inside space. It is a boundary condition used to describe where ordinary spatial extension terminates in the model. A Coherence Membrane is a two-dimensional tension-bearing surface in the visualization. It carries the model's surface-tension term, stores curvature energy, and supports propagation modes. It has no volume in the model; mass-energy is represented by persistent coherence, curvature, or trapped helical modes rather than by substance inside the membrane. Lorentz-invariant photon sector The updated photon sector is the most important change from the older webpage. A literal microscopic lattice through which photons visibly weave would risk producing forbidden effects: preferred directions, birefringence, vacuum dispersion, or frame-dependent light speed. The revised formulation avoids this by treating ordinary photon propagation as propagation through a coarse-grained coherence ground state. This ground state is statistically homogeneous, isotropic, helicity-balanced, and Lorentz invariant at observable scales. LORENTZ PATCH Photons do not propagate through individually observable membrane structures in ordinary flat vacuum. They propagate through the effective coarse-grained vacuum generated by the underlying coherence network. In the unstrained vacuum ground state, the effective metric is the ordinary flat-space metric: g_eff^μν = η^μν Therefore the observable photon dispersion relation remains: ω² = c²k² This means there is no leading-order vacuum dispersion, no preferred frame, no anisotropy, and no usable faster-than-light signal channel in the ordinary flat vacuum limit. 6. What helical propagation means now The older language could be read as saying that each photon physically corkscrews through a literal microscopic lattice. The revised interpretation is more careful. Helical geometry is treated as a fixed invariant of the coherence ground state. Photon energy changes phase accumulation along the propagation mode, not the observable geometry of the path. In ordinary flat vacuum, the effective path remains nondispersive. r = r₀,   p = p₀,   ∂r/∂ω = 0,   ∂p/∂ω = 0 This preserves the geometric intuition of Swarm Field Theory while protecting the theory from the immediate experimental problem of frequency-dependent light speed in empty space. 7. Conditional dispersion Swarm Field Theory does not require ordinary flat vacuum to be dispersive. Instead, deviations from standard photon propagation are expected only when the coherence ground state is disturbed. g_eff^μν = η^μν + ε^μν(Rαβ, ΔI, |ψ|², ∇ψ) Possible correction regimes include: • Curvature: strong gravitational or membrane-curvature regions. • Strain: departures from the relaxed coherence ground state. • Strong-field excitation: nonlinear coherence response at extreme field intensity. • Analogue lattices: engineered membrane systems where quantized defects can be tested directly. 8. Common questions Are you saying spacetime is literally made of membranes? No. The membrane picture is the physical model used to visualize the mathematics. It is a scaffold for reasoning about coherence, tension-flow, curvature, and quantization. Why keep the membrane language if it is not literal? Because it gives the mathematical terms a clear geometric meaning. Surface tension, curvature, aperture, projection, and helical closure are easier to reason about visually than as isolated symbols. Does the theory violate relativity? The revised photon sector is designed not to. In the unstrained ground state, the effective propagation operator is Lorentz invariant and gives the standard nondispersive relation ω² = c²k². Does light secretly travel faster than c? That is not the best current wording. The safer statement is that c is the invariant observable propagation limit. Any deeper lattice or coherence parameters belong to the underlying model and do not provide a usable faster-than-light signal. What are Zero Nodes, then? In the model, Zero Nodes are null boundary conditions. They are not little balls or particles in space. They mark where the geometric description excludes an ordinary propagating interior. What would prove or disprove the framework? The framework becomes vulnerable through its predicted correction regimes: strong-field vacuum response, chromatic micro-shifts near curvature, and analogue membrane defect spectra. It must also continue to reproduce Maxwell behaviour, weak-field gravitational behaviour, and Lorentz-invariant photon propagation in the appropriate limits. The model is not intended as a literal claim that spacetime is visibly constructed from membranes. In the revised Lorentz-invariant formulation, ordinary photons propagate through a coarse-grained, homogeneous, isotropic effective vacuum, giving standard nondispersive light propagation in flat space.
